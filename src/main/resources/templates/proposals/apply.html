ONE TRIP. YOUR RESPONSIBILITY.

Organize one trip.

Anyone with a Waylo account can propose a particular journey. A Platform Admin reviews it. Approval gives you access to manage that trip only; it does not change your account into a global Trip Manager.

Important: This demo collects document type only—no NID number, photo, or passport scan. An admin still needs a real secure off-platform verification process before making actual identity claims.
